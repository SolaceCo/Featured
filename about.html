What this is

FEATURED pulls one piece of Jamaican internet culture every week — a funny comment, a sharp reply, a small business post, a viral clip, a random moment — and gives it a permanent home.

Jamaican internet moves fast. Algorithms bury things within 48 hours. This site exists to slow that down. Each issue gets The Verdict: a short note on why that specific thing earned the spot. The content is not the product. The curation is.

New issues drop weekly. Old issues never expire. The archive is the point.
